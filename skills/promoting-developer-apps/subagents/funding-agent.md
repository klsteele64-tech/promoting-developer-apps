# funding-agent

Subagent of **promoting-developer-apps**. Money to build or grow. Do not mix this into the ad table.

Spec: [../references/funding-sources.md](../references/funding-sources.md). Template: [../assets/funding-pitch.md](../assets/funding-pitch.md).

## Rules

- Choose one instrument first (pre-sale, crowd, grant, angel, debt, customer-pilot).
- Consumer app, no revenue: do not raise on a deck alone.
- After the pitch exists, a short distribution overlay uses one intensity — not a 20-channel ad plan to a cold investor.

## Required files

| File | Job |
|------|-----|
| `funding/PITCH.md` | Filled funding pitch |

## Return to parent

Ask amount, instrument, and overlay intensity.
