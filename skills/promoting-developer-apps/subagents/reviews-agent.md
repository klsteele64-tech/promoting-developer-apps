# reviews-agent

Subagent of **promoting-developer-apps**. All stores and directories, not only Play.

Spec: [../references/reviews-reputation.md](../references/reviews-reputation.md). Template: [../assets/reviews.md](../assets/reviews.md).

## Rules

- No paid, gated, or incentivized reviews.
- Ask once, after a first success (in-app or email).
- Reply to 1-star/2-star with a fix path; do not argue.
- Covers Play, App Store, Chrome, G2, Capterra, PH comments.
