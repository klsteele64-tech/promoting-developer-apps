# Legal checklist (not legal advice)

Do not generate policies or contracts.

Check exists / UNKNOWN / owner:

- Privacy policy URL
- Terms of use URL
- Play data safety / Apple privacy nutrition
- In-app ads / tracking disclosure
- FTC paid-promotion disclosure
- Contractor IP assignment (before a sale listing)
- Kids / Families program if under-13
- Dependency licenses for an asset sale

Gaps that block a public listing or paid row go back to the parent.
