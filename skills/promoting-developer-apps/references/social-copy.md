# Social copy

One fact sheet. One CTA. UTM on every link.

| Piece | Length | Rule |
|-------|--------|------|
| PH first comment | 80–150 words | Maker voice, what it does, link, ask for a specific try |
| Show HN | Title + 2–4 sentences | What you built; show the repo/demo; no marketing speak |
| X thread | 8–12 posts | Hook, problem, 3 steps, proof, CTA |
| LinkedIn | 120–200 words | ICP + outcome; one link |

Stagger PH and Show HN unless Blitz. Reddit is value-first or skip.
