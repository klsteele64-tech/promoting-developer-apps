# Four rollout playbooks

Pick one intensity. Write the other three as named alternatives (3–5 lines each), then execute only the chosen playbook.

## 1. Whisper (soft)

**Goal:** Learn, not scale. Ship to people who will tell you the truth.

**Channel cap:** 3 free. 0 paid.

**Sequence (14 days)**
1. Private list: 20–50 people who already have the problem.
2. Closed beta or waitlist with a written feedback prompt.
3. One community you already belong to. No cold launch posts.
4. Daily notes: activation, confusion, "would you pay?"
5. Kill or iterate before any public launch.

**Assets:** short private note, feedback form, changelog for testers.

**Success:** 5+ completed happy paths and a sharper ICP sentence.

**Fail:** Public launch posts, directories, or ads.

## 2. Signal (soft-medium)

**Goal:** Public proof on one stage. One narrative.

**Channel cap:** 6 free. Paid optional, max $500, one channel.

**Sequence (14 days)**
1. Landing + OG + one CTA ready.
2. Docs or 90-second demo exist (run content studio if missing).
3. Pick **one** primary stage: Product Hunt **or** Show HN **or** a niche subreddit/IH — not all three the same day.
4. Two support posts: founder story + how-to.
5. Email existing users the morning of launch.
6. Optional $20–50/day search test on 5 keywords only.

**Success:** A screenshotable public thread + measurable CTA (waitlist or signup).

**Fail:** Splitting the same 24h across PH + HN + Reddit + ads.

## 3. Pulse (medium-hard)

**Goal:** Coordinated 2–6 week push to a known ICP.

**Channel cap:** 10 total, of which at most 4 paid.

**Sequence**
1. Week −1: brief, creative, tracking UTMs, pixel/analytics, support coverage.
2. Week 1: owned + one launch community + search ads if budget ≥ $500.
3. Week 2: one social paid test **or** one newsletter sponsor; publish comparison/vs page.
4. Weeks 3–4: double down on the one paid winner; cut the rest.
5. Weekly retro: CAC proxy, activation, qualitative objections.

**Budget split default:** 50% search, 25% social or Reddit, 25% sponsorship.

**Success:** Repeatable acquisition at or below a stated ceiling.

**Fail:** More than 4 paid channels at once; no kill criteria.

## 4. Blitz (hard and fast)

**Goal:** Saturate a launch window. Only if demand is already proven (users, revenue, or waitlist).

**Channel cap:** 14 total. Paid is expected.

**Sequence (10–14 days)**
1. T−7: press/indie kit, creator briefs, ad accounts funded, landing load-tested.
2. T−3: seed users scheduled; PH/HN assets frozen.
3. T0: owned email + social + launch community + search + social ads same day.
4. T+1–3: creator posts, newsletter drops, retargeting.
5. T+7: cut losers; write a post-mortem even if it worked.

**Guardrails**
- Do not Blitz a first-week product.
- Disclosure on every paid creator.
- Support SLA: replies same day or downgrade to Pulse.
- Brand-safety: no fake scarcity, no purchased upvotes.

**Success:** Spike that retains ≥ 20% of new actives at day 7, or a funding/sale event the campaign was built for.

**Fail:** Spend without proof; multi-channel dump with no tracking.

## Choosing when the user says "recommend"

| Evidence | Default |
|----------|---------|
| No users, no waitlist | Whisper |
| Demo works, thin proof | Signal |
| ICP named, some traction | Pulse |
| Traction + deadline (PH day, fundraise, sale) | Blitz |

If evidence is mixed, pick the **softer** mode.
