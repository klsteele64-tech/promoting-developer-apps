# Interview intake

Use when product facts are missing. Ask in this order. Stop when you can write the brief.

1. What does a user do in the first 60 seconds?
2. Who fails today without this product?
3. What is the substitute they already use?
4. What is free vs paid, and what is the price?
5. What proof exists (users, revenue, stars, waitlist, logos)?
6. What must not be claimed?
7. Geography and language?
8. Is the goal users, revenue, funding, or a sale?
9. If Android / Play: package name or Play URL, current track (internal/closed/open/production), countries, IAP or ads in the app?

Record answers as bullet facts. Do not polish into marketing copy until the brief exists.
